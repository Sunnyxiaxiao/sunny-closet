## Fabric & Tech
- Waterproof rating >20,000 mmH₂O (lab tested, GB/T 4744-2013)
- Water resistance grade 4–5 (lab tested, GB/T 4745-2012)
- Breathability >15,000 g/m²/24h (lab tested, JIS L1099-2021)
- Colorfastness grade 4–5 (lab tested, GB/T 5713-2013)
- 3-layer fabric: dense shell that resists water, oil and abrasion; waterproof breathable membrane; soft fleece lining
- All seams sealed with 18 mm tape to keep out snowmelt and wind
- Light, warm fleece lining, rated by the brand to −30°C (−22°F)

## Design Details
- Underarm and inner-thigh vents to cool down fast
- Drawcord adjusters throughout for a custom fit
- Extended cuffs keep snow out; adjustable hook-and-loop cuffs fit easily over gloves
- Two roomy side pockets, plus a ski pass pocket on the inner arm — just raise your arm to scan
- Inner mesh pocket on the jacket for your goggles
- Snow skirts at the jacket hem and pant cuffs
- Adjustable bib straps; zippered pant cuffs make it easy to get on over ski boots
